Stock perp funding on Hyperliquid

trade.xyz (HIP-3) perps, hourly funding annualized. Positive means longs pay and shorts receive.

Open = NYSE regular session, 9:30–16:00 New York time. The 9:00–10:00 hour counts as open; holidays and half-days follow the NYSE calendar.

Rolling funding by window

How the numbers are built

  • Source: Hyperliquid fundingHistory and candleSnapshot for the xyz DEX, captured once at the snapshot time above.
  • Annualized rate = average hourly funding rate in the window (and bucket) × 8,760.
  • Shorts received = share of hourly prints with a positive rate. A zero-rate hour does not count.
  • Windows trail back from the latest print. A window longer than the market's history uses all of it and is marked “partial”.
  • Rolling lines show the same average over the trailing window at every bar close; a gap means the window held no hours in that bucket (for example, open-hours 24h over a weekend).
  • Hyperliquid only serves the latest 5,000 hourly candles, so the 1h price chart begins in March 2026; 4h and 1D cover the full history.
